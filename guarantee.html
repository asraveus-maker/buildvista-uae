Trust, guarantee, and dispute policy

Clear conditions for a clearer relationship.

BuildVista can stand behind the platform service it delivers. It cannot replace the responsibility of a licensed architect, engineer, contractor, or supplier.

1Before work begins

A customer may request cancellation before a paid platform service begins, subject to the selected package terms. Any confirmed scope, revision limit, and timing should be visible before the customer proceeds.

2Platform service quality

If BuildVista fails to deliver a paid platform service or loses a required project document, the proposed remedy is correction, re-performance, or a service refund according to the final terms.

3Professional responsibility

Final construction work, materials, defects, warranties, approvals, permits, and engineering responsibility belong to the customer’s separate contract with the selected licensed professional.

4Transparent ranking

Sponsored listings, professional ranking factors, review verification, and any success fee should be disclosed. BuildVista should not present paid placement as an independent recommendation.

What BuildVista does not promise

No shortcuts around professional responsibility.

The platform does not approve structural plans, obtain permits, certify construction quality, or guarantee an indicative price as a final construction cost.
