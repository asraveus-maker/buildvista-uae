A commercial BuildVista platform would handle identity, drawings, budgets, payment references, documents, and project conversations with care.

Prototype boundary

This static course website does not create accounts, store form submissions, connect to a payment gateway, or upload documents. The controls below are the proposed commercial approach.

01Collect only what is needed

Project location, size, room priorities, budget range, and contact details should be collected only when they support a clear project step or customer request.

02Role-based access

Customers, professionals, support staff, and administrators should see only the projects, documents, and profile information needed for their role.

03Protect data in transit and at rest

A commercial implementation should use encrypted connections and encrypted storage, with secure backups and monitoring for sensitive project information.

04Respect the record

Customers should be able to correct inaccurate profile or project information. Reviews should be linked to real interaction and professional changes should be documented.

05Payment minimization

BuildVista should use an approved payment provider and store only necessary references. The platform should never ask a customer to paste a card number into a form or message.

06Clear retention

Documents, messages, and verification records should have defined retention periods and deletion or export procedures that are explained to the user.

Security and ethics

Good controls protect more than data.

They also protect customer expectations. BuildVista should show cost ranges as assumptions, identify sponsored listings, avoid fictitious reviews, and state the limits of its role around approvals and construction quality.
