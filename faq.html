Ordering and project briefs

What does BuildVista help me do?

BuildVista helps organize an early home-building or renovation idea into a structured brief that can support clearer conversations with designers, consultants, contractors, and suppliers.

Which package should I choose first?

Choose Design Preview if you are still exploring a direction, Design + Estimate if you want selections connected to a preliminary range, or Verified BuildMatch if you want a standardized professional comparison.

Can I change my service package after submitting?

In this prototype, you can submit a new brief with a different package. A commercial service would confirm changes and any cancellation or re-performance terms before work begins.

Do I need final architectural drawings?

No. The intake is designed for an early idea. It is not a substitute for approved architectural, structural, engineering, or permit documentation.

Delivery and professional matching

How is the service delivered?

Design previews, project briefs, estimates, comparison tables, and status updates are delivered digitally through the website and future customer accounts. Physical construction work and materials come from the selected professional or supplier.

Which UAE locations are in scope?

The proposal focuses the initial service on Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Al Ain, and Sharjah so that verification processes, currency, and customer expectations can be handled consistently.

Does BuildVista guarantee a contractor’s work?

No. BuildVista can structure discovery and document verification, but the customer’s separate contract with a licensed professional governs construction quality, defects, timelines, warranties, and final responsibility.

Returns, cancellation, and service quality

How do I start a cancellation or service claim?

Use the Contact page and include your project reference, selected package, and the issue. A real implementation would review whether work has started and apply the published service terms.

What happens if a paid platform service is not delivered?

Depending on the final terms, the remedy could be correction, re-performance, or a service refund. Physical material returns and contractor defects are managed under the customer’s separate contract.

Can I return a material sample or construction product?

BuildVista does not sell or physically deliver materials in this prototype. Any material order would be subject to the selected supplier’s written terms, lead time, condition, and return rules.

Payment, privacy, and trust

Are the prices real?

The AED 299, AED 999, and AED 1,999 package prices are indicative display prices from the proposal. No card details are requested and no real payment is processed by this course prototype.

Why does the order form ask for a payment preference?

The assignment requires a payment method choice to be displayed. The form only records a preference for demonstration and does not connect to a payment gateway.

What information would BuildVista protect?

A commercial platform would protect identity details, drawings, budgets, payment references, documents, and project communications with role-based access, encryption, backups, and monitoring. See the Privacy & Security page for the proposed controls.

How are professional profiles reviewed?

The proposed workflow separates self-reported profile information from evidence such as licensing, service categories, project records, response time, warranties, and verified reviews. The prototype shows the process without claiming live verification.
